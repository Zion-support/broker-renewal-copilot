# Broker Renewal Copilot

**Renewal pipeline intelligence, client outreach drafts and quote comparison for brokers & MGAs.** Part of the [Zion AI App Network](https://ziontechgroup.com/apps/network.html) (Batch 101 — Insurance & Risk AI) by [Zion Tech Group](https://ziontechgroup.com/).

## What it does
- **Renewal pipeline** — 120/90/60-day radar with at-risk account scoring and next-best-action.
- **Outreach drafts** — personalized renewal emails and meeting briefs per client.
- **Quote comparison** — normalize carrier quotes side-by-side: coverage gaps, exclusions, price.
- **Cross-sell signals** — identify under-insured lines using book-of-business analytics.
